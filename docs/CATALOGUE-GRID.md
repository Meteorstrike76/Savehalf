# Shared catalogue browsing

Catalogue advertisement cards and collected product cards now share the main grid, global text/price/category filters and title-derived product-type filters. Default Retailer mix interleaves the two sources and uses retailer diversity within the advertisement preview. Selecting price sorting orders the known advertisement candidates before pagination and the loaded combined rows; additional collected pages still require loading. Unit-price and evidenced-reduction filters exclude advertisements without the required evidence.

Advertised price, source link, region, per-kg units and expiry remain visible. Advertisements are not converted into saved/compared/meal-plan Deal records or verified reductions. Dated uploads remain visible through their advertised end date in the edition timezone (NT: Darwin, VIC: Melbourne, NSW and unverified/national: Sydney). Unknown-end uploads retain a 24-hour review limit. ALDI PDF exclusion remains unchanged. More retailer selection includes IGA, IKEA and Officeworks; collected-source category counts are explicitly separate from advertisement totals.

Catalogue crops use the original page's pixel dimensions and SVG view boxes so images retain their proportions. Full-page-only evidence uses a labelled product illustration with a link to the original catalogue rather than displaying an unreadably tiny page as a product photo. Source images and their checksums are unchanged. Shared image wells are 220px desktop and 180px narrow-screen, with contained product photos.

Browsing labels are inferred from titles and may overlap, such as frozen turkey appearing under Meat & seafood and Frozen. They are not verified dietary, allergen or nutrition classifications. Fresh excludes frozen/tinned produce and cleaning-product names. Tests cover these negative cases and assert that catalogue advertisements and collected products share a parent grid while half-price mode excludes unverified advertisements.
