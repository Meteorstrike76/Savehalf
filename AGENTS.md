# SaveHalf project rules

- Use free-tier services within their documented quotas. Do not activate billing, paid upgrades, paid integrations, paid APIs or paid add-ons.
- The owner approved the scoped grounded deal assistant, quarantined data-quality checks and budget planner using Groq Free and Cloudflare Workers AI Free. Keep these optional, server-only, quota-bounded and disabled until credentials and free account limits are verified. Never route to paid models or activate billing.
- Prefer local browser calculations, built-in recipe templates and user-initiated links to publicly accessible third-party resources.
- The retired meal-recipe endpoint must not call any provider or read provider credentials.
- Keep scrape requests bounded, record blocked/empty results honestly, and never fabricate products, previous prices, savings or complete catalogue coverage.
- Keep signup focus stable. Preserve Google and email Auth redirects and respect Supabase RLS.
- Never commit secrets or put service-role credentials in frontend code.

- Owner approved N1–N10 integration batch on 22 October 2026: bounded Supabase flags/reports, GitHub quality/evals/Lighthouse/axe/Playwright/Dependabot, Turnstile, Better Stack Uptime, existing provider router upgrades, TanStack Query, Zod, Open Food Facts, native/ZXing barcode input, Cloudflare Web Analytics replacement, native UI/SVG/build-time share assets. Keep deferred maps, OCR, voice, push, other providers and extra UI frameworks out. New service accounts with card requirements must not be activated. See docs/SERVICES-REGISTRY.md for pending activation gates.
